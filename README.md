## Project title
Executive Business Intelligence Hub | Excel Dashboard

## Role
Data Analyst & Excel Dashboard Developer

## Project summary
Designed and built an Excel-based executive business intelligence hub that transforms a structured sales dataset into a management-oriented reporting system. The workbook separates raw data from the cleaned analytical layer and presents executive, sales, customer, product, and management-insight views through a consistent dashboard interface.

## Dataset
- 500 sales/order records
- 20 customers
- 10 products
- 3 product categories
- 500 unique order IDs
- Data period: 2 January 2026 to 15 September 2026
- 490 records marked OK by the workbook's data-quality layer
- 10 records flagged for source-data review

## Headline metrics shown by the workbook
- Revenue: KSh 3,597,200
- Profit: KSh 1,444,250
- Profit margin: 40.1%

## Workbook architecture
1. HOME — navigation and system overview
2. EXECUTIVE — management dashboard and business-health signals
3. SALES — revenue, monthly performance, payment mix and category performance
4. CUSTOMERS — customer ranking and concentration analysis
5. PRODUCTS — product ranking, margin and contribution analysis
6. INSIGHTS — management readout and recommended actions
7. CONTROL — KPI definitions and data-quality notes
8. DATA_CLEAN — analytical layer with calculated metrics and quality status
9. DATA_RAW — source-data layer
10. CALCS — supporting monthly calculations

## Important scope note
This is a personal portfolio project using sample data. It is not presented as paid client work. The workbook itself contains a control note that sample data should be replaced with validated operational data for production use.

## What makes the project portfolio-worthy
The goal was not only to make charts. The workbook is structured as a small business intelligence system: source data is separated from the analytical layer, business metrics are defined, data-quality issues are surfaced, multiple analytical views are provided, and the final Insights page translates numbers into management actions.
